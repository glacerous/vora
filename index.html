📖 Tips for best results

  • 🎥 Record a slow, steady pan around the object (~20-30 sec). Faster = more blur = worse result.
  • 💡 Diffuse indoor light (near a window) beats direct sunlight — avoids harsh shadows.
  • 📐 Walk a complete circle. Add a second pass tilted slightly downward for top coverage.
  • 🔒 Tap-hold in your camera app to lock exposure (AE/AF Lock) before recording.
  • 🖥️ Reflective screens, glass, and metal will show floater artifacts — this is a known limitation of 3DGS.
  • 💰 Each reconstruction costs ~$0.05–0.15 on your Modal A10G free credits ($30 budget).
